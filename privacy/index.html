1. What we collect ourselves

Nothing. ProductSeal does not run a server for Rampstorm and does not include any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. The game's own code does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

That means we do not collect or receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account identifier (there is no sign-in);
  • your location (the game does not ask for location permission);
  • your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera (never requested);
  • your advertising ID, IP address or other device identifiers;
  • your game progress, scores, settings or purchase history.

The only time we receive personal data is if you email us. We then have your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply and to handle your request. See section 8 for how long we keep it.

3. Advertising: Google AdMob and consent

Rampstorm shows one kind of ad: a rewarded ad that you choose to start, usually a short video and sometimes an interactive ad, in exchange for in-game credits. There are no ads when you open the game, between flights or during a flight. Watching ads is optional and never required to play.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Consent is handled by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Both are made by Google LLC and run inside the app. When the game checks your consent status and when you watch an ad, Google may collect and process the following data. This data is shared with Google: Google uses it under its own policies and acts as an independent controller of it. It is not sent to us, and it is not shared with anyone else by us.

4. Your ad choices and how to change them

  • In the game. Open Settings and tap Privacy choices to see or change your consent at any time. The same button is available where rewarded ads are offered. If you are in a US state with a privacy law, this is also where you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising.
  • On your device. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. On most devices it is under Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your Android version. Deleting it stops personalised ads based on that ID across your device.
  • With Google. You can manage ad personalisation for your Google Account at https://myadcenter.google.com/.
  • Simply do not watch ads. Every ad in Rampstorm is optional. If you never start a rewarded ad, no ad is shown. Google's consent tool may still check your consent status with Google when the game starts, which involves your IP address and device information.

5. Purchases: Google Play Billing

Hangar Kits are one-time purchases processed entirely by Google Play using the Google Play Billing Library.

  • Google handles your payment. We never see or receive your card or bank details, billing address, name or email address.
  • The game receives from Google Play only the purchase status: which products your Google account owns, whether each purchase is completed or pending, and a purchase reference code. It uses this only to unlock what you bought, and it keeps the record on your device.
  • We do not run a server that checks purchases, so no purchase record about you is kept by us. Google keeps your order history in your Google account under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy. If a purchase is refunded, Google Play stops reporting it and the game removes that kit the next time it checks.

7. Children

Rampstorm is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Because the game has no sign-in, we do not ask for anyone's age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal data (for example in an email), contact us at dev@productseal.com and we will delete it. To remove game data from the device, uninstall the game or use the delete option described below. To reset data used for ads, reset or delete the device's advertising ID as described in section 4.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws give you rights over your personal data. In plain terms:

Europe and the UK (GDPR and UK GDPR)

  • Access and portability: ask what data is held about you and get a copy. In the game, Account > Export my data copies your locally saved profile data (everything except your settings, which you can see on the Settings screen) to your device's clipboard, so you can paste it where you choose.
  • Correction and deletion: have wrong data corrected, or data deleted.
  • Objection and restriction: object to or limit how your data is used.
  • Withdraw consent at any time through Settings > Privacy choices. This does not affect what happened before you withdrew it.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

For ads, the legal basis is your consent where consent is required. Google explains its own legal bases in the Google Privacy Policy. For emails you send us, we rely on our legitimate interest in answering you.

United States (state privacy laws, such as California's)

  • Know and access the personal information held about you.
  • Delete or correct it.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, using Settings > Privacy choices in the game or by deleting your advertising ID.
  • You will not be treated differently for using these rights.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Google's use of advertising data may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws, which is why the opt-out above exists.

How to use your rights

Email dev@productseal.com. Because we do not hold your game or ad data, most requests are answered by the in-game and device controls above, and requests about data Google holds are best made to Google through its own tools. We will still reply to every request, explain what applies to you, and delete any emails from you on request. We answer within the time the law requires (normally one month under GDPR, or 45 days under US state laws).

10. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage and protected by Android's app sandbox. Google's ads and billing software encrypts data it sends over the network. We do not store your data on any server, which removes a whole category of risk. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock.

11. International transfers

We do not transfer your data anywhere. Google may process ad and payment data in countries other than yours, using the safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to this policy
