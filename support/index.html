1. Get help

Email dev@productseal.com. It helps if you include:

  • your device model and Android version;
  • the game version (shown on the game's Google Play page, under "About this game");
  • what happened and what you expected;
  • for a purchase problem, the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play order history and your receipt email).

You can also include the Support ID shown on the game's Account screen. It is a random code created on your device and does not identify you. Please never send us your password or payment card details. We will never ask for them.

2. Restore purchases

Hangar Kits are one-time purchases linked to the Google account you used to buy them, so they can come back on a new install or a new device.

  1. Make sure your device is online and the Google Play Store is signed in to the same Google account you used to buy the kit.
  2. Open Rampstorm. The game checks with Google Play for your purchases when it starts and when you return to it.
  3. If a kit is still missing, tap Restore purchases in the Store or on the Account screen.
  4. If it still does not appear, check the purchase is listed in your Google Play order history and is not pending or refunded, then email us with the order number.

Note: game progress, such as Coins (earned or bought in coin packs), XP, completed contracts and items bought with Coins, is saved only on your device and cannot be restored after uninstalling or changing devices. Only purchased Hangar Kits are restored.

3. Refunds and purchase problems

Purchases are processed by Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. To request a refund, see Google Play's refund help page. If a payment went through but the kit did not unlock, first try the steps in Restore purchases, then email us.

4. Delete your local data

We do not keep your game data on any server. Everything is on your device, and you can delete it yourself:

  • In the game: open Account and tap Delete profile, then confirm. This permanently erases your career, in-game balances, owned items and cosmetics on this device.
  • In Android settings: go to Settings > Apps > Rampstorm > Storage and tap Clear storage (the exact names vary by device). This also resets your settings and your ad consent choice.
  • Uninstall the game. This removes all of its data from the device.

These steps cannot be undone. They do not remove Hangar Kits you bought, which stay with your Google account. Before deleting, you can copy your profile data to your device's clipboard with Account > Export my data and paste it somewhere safe.

If you emailed us and want that conversation deleted, email dev@productseal.com. To manage data Google holds for ads, see the next section.

5. Change your ad choices

  • In the game: open Settings and tap Privacy choices (also available where rewarded ads are offered). You can change or withdraw your consent, and in US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising.
  • On your device: reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your Android version.
  • With Google: manage ad personalisation at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

All ads in Rampstorm are optional rewarded ads, usually short videos. Declining consent or never watching ads does not affect free play. More detail is in the privacy policy.
